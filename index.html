Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Is Divide free?

Yes. Divide is free to use.

Do I have to link my bank?

No. Divide never connects to your bank. You add each card by hand with just the issuer, last four digits, limit, and balance.

Does Divide see my card number?

Divide is read-only and never stores full card numbers. It never processes a payment. It only tells you which card to use.

How are scores calculated?

Every card gets a score for the purchase in front of you. Divide weighs the reward rate for that store type, how much of the card's limit you'd be using after you pay, and your available credit. The highest score wins, and the reason is shown right next to it.

Which cards does it know?

Popular US rewards cards from Chase, American Express, Citi, Capital One, and more, with reward rates checked against each issuer's own pages. New cards are added all the time.
