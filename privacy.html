01Who we are

Divide LLC ("Divide," "we," "us") makes the Divide browser extension and runs this website. Divide is based in the United States. This policy covers the extension, this website, and any emails we send you.

Divide is an advisory tool. It suggests which of your cards to use at checkout. It does not process payments, move money, apply for credit, or make purchases for you.

02What we collect

When you sign up for updates on this website

  • Your email address.
  • Whether you want launch updates or already use Divide.

When you use the Divide extension

  • Card details you add: the card issuer and name, the last four digits, the credit limit, and the balance. If you add cards by hand, this is the only card information we have.
  • If you choose to connect an account: balances, credit limits, and due dates for your linked cards, so recommendations stay current. You sign in to your bank or issuer through a secure connection provider. Divide never sees or stores your bank password.
  • Checkout details: when you're on a checkout page, the extension reads the store name, the purchase total, and the store type so it can score your cards for that purchase.
  • Your choices: which recommendations you accept or change, so Divide's suggestions get more accurate for you over time.
  • Account details: the email you use to sign in, and your settings and preferences.
What we never collect: your full card number, your card's security code (CVV), your bank password, or the items in your cart.

When you visit this website

This website doesn't use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Like any website, our hosting provider automatically receives basic technical information when your browser loads the page, such as your IP address and browser type. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which also receives your IP address when it delivers them.

03How we use it

  • To recommend the best card for each purchase and explain why.
  • To show how a purchase would change each card's balance and utilization.
  • To improve the accuracy of recommendations, including learning from the recommendations you accept or change.
  • To send the emails you asked for, like the launch announcement and product updates.
  • To keep Divide secure, fix problems, and respond to you when you contact us.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We don't use your information to make credit decisions, and we don't sell it to lenders, advertisers, or data brokers.

05How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as you use Divide or stay on our email list. When you delete your account, we delete your card details and recommendation history. When you unsubscribe, we stop emailing you and remove you from our list. We may keep a small amount of information longer when the law requires it.

06How we protect it

Your information is encrypted when it travels and when it's stored. Access is limited to the people and services that need it to run Divide. No system is perfectly secure, but we work hard to protect your data and will tell you promptly if a breach affects you.

07Your choices and rights

  • Add cards by hand: you never have to connect a bank account to use Divide.
  • Edit or remove cards anytime in the extension.
  • Delete everything in Settings, or email us and we'll do it for you.
  • Unsubscribe from emails with the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Ask for a copy of the information we have about you.

Depending on where you live, including California and other US states, you may have additional rights to know, access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to not be treated differently for using them. We honor these requests for everyone. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

08Children

Divide is built for adults who manage their own credit cards. It isn't meant for anyone under 18, and we don't knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

09Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll let you know by email or in the extension before it takes effect.
